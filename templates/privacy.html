Privacy

pleskal is a small, non-commercial project. This page explains what personal data it handles and why. Questions or requests: hello.pleskal@proton.me.

Cookies

pleskal uses no tracking or advertising cookies. It sets only the cookies it needs to work: a security token and, when you log in, a session cookie. Your browser also keeps a short history of the pages you filter so the back button works; it never leaves your device.

Visitor statistics

To understand how the site is used, the server counts page views, the website you came from, and the searches and filters used, as daily totals. Your IP address is anonymized and used only to count unique visitors; it's never stored.

Accounts

If you have an account, pleskal stores your email address, the profile details you add (display name, bio, website), the events you publish, and who invited you. Your email address is never shown publicly; your profile and published events are. Login attempts are logged with IP address and browser to protect accounts from break-ins. You can delete your account at any time from your account settings, with or without the events you published.
