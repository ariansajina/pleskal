Privacy

pleskal is a small, non-commercial project. This page explains what personal data it handles and why. Questions or requests: hello.pleskal@proton.me.

Cookies

pleskal uses no tracking or advertising cookies. It sets only the cookies it needs to work: a security token (csrftoken) and, when you log in, a session cookie (sessionid). Your browser also keeps a short history of the pages you filter so the back button works; it never leaves your device.

Visitor statistics

To understand how the site is used, the server counts page views, the website you came from, and the searches and filters used, as daily totals. To count unique visitors per day, your IP address and browser are combined with a random value that is deleted every night and turned into a code that can't be reversed. Your IP address is never stored, and visits on different days can't be linked. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, you aren't counted as a visitor.

Accounts

If you have an account, pleskal stores your email address, the profile details you add (display name, bio, website), the events you publish, and who invited you. Your email address is never shown publicly; your profile and published events are. Login attempts are logged with IP address and browser to protect accounts from break-ins.

Emails (address verification, password resets) are sent through Resend. Once you verify your email address, it and your display name are added to pleskal's contact list at Resend.

You can delete your account at any time from your account settings, with or without the events you published. To be removed from the Resend contact list as well, send an email.

Services pleskal relies on

  • Railway: hosting and database.
  • Cloudflare R2: event images and database backups.
  • Resend: sending email.
  • Sentry: error reports, set up not to include personal data.
  • OpenStreetMap: maps load from openstreetmap.org, which receives your IP address.
  • Ko-fi: the donation button loads from ko-fi.com, which receives your IP address and applies its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it's used, by emailing hello.pleskal@proton.me. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet.
