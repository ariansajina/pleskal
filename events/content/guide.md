A field guide to Copenhagen's dance and performance art scene — the institutions, companies, collectives, festivals, spaces and voices that make it move. Each entry links out to the original source; descriptions are short summaries, translated to English where the original is in Danish.

## Institutions

### [Den Danske Scenekunstskole (The Danish National School of Performing Arts)](https://ddsks.dk/en)

Denmark's leading state-funded higher-education institution for the performing arts, offering bachelor's and master's programmes in dance, choreography, acting, musical theatre and stage production. Its Copenhagen campus sits on the former naval island of Holmen, equipped with dance and theatre halls, a stage tower and production workshops. Here emerging talents across every performing-arts discipline are trained into leading professional artists grounded in both practice and theory.

### [AFUK – Akademiet for Utæmmet Kreativitet (Academy of Untamed Creativity)](https://www.afuk.dk/)

AFUK is a Copenhagen creative hub built around contemporary circus and physical-visual performance, housed in a former tram depot in Sydhavn. Tracing its roots to the 1986 "Gøglerskolen," it runs year-long creative programmes — from circus to musical theatre — designed especially for young people who haven't thrived in conventional education, letting them learn by doing and rebuild their confidence. Its stage, AFUK Scene, presents Danish and international guest performances within contemporary circus.

### [Dansehallerne](https://dansehallerne.dk/en/about/)

Dansehallerne is Northern Europe's largest centre for contemporary dance and a national hub for dance and choreography, presenting and co-producing Danish and international work for all ages. Since 2024 it has occupied a dramatic new home in Copenhagen's Carlsberg City District — a former brewery boiler house transformed into what has been called "something near a cathedral for dance." Alongside performances, talks and workshops, it offers rehearsal and training spaces, a dance library and dance information services.

### [HAUT](https://www.hautscene.dk/en/about)

HAUT is a cross-aesthetic, international performing-arts organisation in Copenhagen focused on artistic development and research. It creates room for unexpected encounters between art forms, perspectives and people through experimentation, conversation, collaboration and risk-taking. HAUT offers artists residencies with studio space and paid time to expand their somatic, performative and scenic practice, and is based at Thoravej 29.

### [Sort/Hvid](https://sort-hvid.dk/en/profile/)

Sort/Hvid (Black/White) is one of Copenhagen's most agenda-setting theatres, producing genre-crossing work that examines current social taboos and issues. Based in a renovated slaughterhouse in the Meatpacking District since 2017, it collaborates with everyone from independent performance groups to major arts institutions. Selected by the Danish Arts Council as a development stage for music theatre, it aims to invent — and dissolve — new genres; since 2023 it has been led by scenographer and director Nathalie Mellbye.

### [Sydhavn Teater](https://sydhavnteater.dk/om-os)

Sydhavn Teater has grown from a small project theatre into a district theatre rooted in one of Copenhagen's fast-growing neighbourhoods. Founded on an idea from 2012 and made a small city theatre under the City of Copenhagen in 2017, it works as a melting pot for artistic innovation with lived experience at its core — staging new work across dance, performance and theatre, both on stages and in urban spaces. In 2026 it gains a permanent home in the former Østre Kapel at Vestre Kirkegård.

## Companies

### [Uppercut Danseteater (Uppercut Dance Theater)](https://en.uppercutdanseteater.dk/)

Founded in 1982, Uppercut is a diverse, international Copenhagen dance company that has made hard-hitting work for adult, young and the very youngest audiences for over forty years. Its performances fuse modern dance, breakdance and theatre, drawing on the dancers' backgrounds in capoeira, breaking, acrobatics and contemporary dance. Now led by Stephanie Thomasen and Mark Philip, the company has earned several Reumert nominations and other awards.

### [Dansk Danseteater (Danish Dance Theatre)](https://www.danskdanseteater.dk/en/about/)

Founded in 1981 and based at the Royal Danish Opera House, Danish Dance Theatre is Denmark's national company for contemporary dance and one of the most prominent in Europe. With a full-time ensemble of carefully chosen dancers, it tours internationally to critical and popular acclaim. Under choreographer Tim Rushton (artistic director 2001–2018) it grew into a multi-award-winning company known for emotionally-led work mixing live music, striking light and design.

## Cooperatives

### [KOMMA Performance Productions](https://kommaproductions.dk/about-1)

KOMMA is a Copenhagen production cooperative in the independent field of dance and choreography, run by and for self-producing artists. It produces and co-produces performances, workshops and festivals while developing sustainable working models — including collective salary agreements, shared knowledge platforms and rotating, member-driven working groups. Its roughly 17 members have realised projects backed by the Danish Arts Foundation, the Nordic Council of Ministers and other funders.

### [Dance Cooperative](https://www.instagram.com/dance.cooperative/)

Dance Cooperative is an artist-run platform and studio organised by a group of performing artists in Copenhagen. It vibrates between being a work studio, a performance venue and a collaborative political voice, supporting intersectional practices, work and performance by professional artists. The self-organised collective serves as a hub for residencies, rehearsals and showings.

### [DANSEatelier](https://www.danseatelier.dk/)

DANSEatelier is both a place and a group of around eleven colleagues, dancers and friends working in dance in Copenhagen and internationally. Collectively run since 2015, it grew from a need for continuity, knowledge-sharing and mutual artistic and social support around dance and choreography. Its house-artist programme "Space Away" (2024–2028) invites artists through open calls to share the studio for extended periods — whether for research, rehearsal or simply time in a space dedicated to dance.

## Festivals

### [Toaster](https://toastercph.dk/om/)

Toaster is a Copenhagen platform presenting dance and performance work rooted in dance, text, costume, sound, light, music and space. It collaborates with partners such as CPH STAGE, ILT Festival and REVOLVER to present performances, embracing the live, here-and-now nature of performance as time chosen to be spent together.

### [YES – dance biennale](https://yes-dancebiennale.dk/en-gb/om)

YES is a new, large-scale dance biennale that fills a full month with the most distinctive contemporary dance and choreography from Copenhagen's scene, staged across venues and public spaces throughout the city (next edition 26 August–28 September 2026). It is a close collaboration between Danish Dance Theatre, Dansehallerne, Copenhagen Dances and Sydhavn Teater, created to unite the field and capture the momentum the art form is experiencing both nationally and internationally.

### [IPAF – International Performance Art Festival (Warehouse9)](https://warehouse9.dk/ipaf/)

Run by Warehouse9 — a queer performance-art venue, gallery and social space founded in 2007 in Copenhagen's former Meatpacking District — IPAF has since 2008 been a biennial gathering for local and international queer performance, discussion and activism. The festival unfolds across the city with genre-blurring live art, choreography, music, performance installations, residencies, talks and parties. Its work engages with identity politics, sexual expression and body representation.

### [Detour / Contact Festival](http://www.detourfestival.com/contactfestival/)

Detour is a Copenhagen dance festival whose Contact Festival strand centres on contact improvisation — a movement practice built on presence, listening, trust and a heightened awareness of one's own body, partners, space and gravity. It brings together the local and international contact-improvisation community around jams, workshops and shared dancing.

### [CPH STAGE](https://www.cphstage.dk/en/publikum/om-festivalen)

CPH STAGE is Denmark's largest festival for professional theatre, held each year across Copenhagen and Frederiksberg over roughly ten days in late May and early June. Running since 2013, it offers more than 100 shows and events — from grand dramas and intense monologues to topical debates, satire and dance — spanning the full breadth of contemporary Danish theatre alongside international touring highlights. Its curated "SELECTED" programme restages standout Danish and international performances.

## Spaces

### [Matriklen](https://matriklen.wordpress.com/)

Matriklen is a community and cultural venue on Amager (Frankrigsgade 10–12) that hosts events, workshops and other activities and can be rented for gatherings. Its WordPress site serves as the hub for news about availability, bookings and what's happening in the space.

### [Forsøgsstationen (The Experimental Station)](https://www.forsoegsstationen.dk/en/experimental-station/)

Forsøgsstationen is a Copenhagen venue dedicated to research, development and training in the professional performing arts, housed in the 1920s former Boulevard cinema on Vesterbro. It offers a place where performing artists can train, experiment and try out research risk-free, with ongoing training programmes, workshops and a library of theatre and arts books and films. It embraces acting, musical theatre, opera, performance, physical and visual theatre, dance and circus, for audiences of all ages.

### [Christianshavns Beboerhus](https://beboerhus.dk/historie)

Christianshavns Beboerhus is a local, self-owning culture house formed in the 1970s, when residents occupied the old Kofoeds School building rather than see it turned into a parking lot. Located at Dronningensgade 34, it is run almost entirely by volunteers on grassroots-democratic principles, hosting concerts, communal dinners and a wide range of resident-initiated activities.

## Blogs

### [bastard](https://bastard.blog/info/)

bastard is an independent Danish platform and forum for experimental performing arts, performance art, choreography and cross-disciplinary practice — and a publisher of performance texts — working to develop a language for art forms that challenge genre. It aims to strengthen art criticism in Denmark by supporting diverse critical voices and fair pay for critics. Through the collaborative project "bastard nordic," with editorial offices in Stockholm and Oslo, it has expanded into a Scandinavian platform for dance and performance criticism.
