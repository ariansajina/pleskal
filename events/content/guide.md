
A list of institutions, collectives, companies, festivals, and spaces that shape and sustain Copenhagen's contemporary dance and performance art landscape.

Inspired by Emma Warren's _Document Your Culture_ pamphlet, the list aims to document and make accessible on a best-effort basis, and not present an authoritative curation. Suggestions, corrections, and additions are welcome, reach out at: hello.pleskal@proton.me

## Institutions

### [Den Danske Scenekunstskole (DDSKS)](https://ddsks.dk/en)

The Danish National School of Performing Arts (Den Danske Scenekunstskole / DDSKS) is Denmark's leading state-run higher-education institution for the performing arts, offering bachelor's and master's degrees in dance, choreography, musical theatre, acting, directing, dramaturgy and stage design across campuses in Copenhagen, Aarhus, and Holstebro. The school trains the next generation of performing artists with an international scope and is a key pipeline of choreographers and dancers into the Copenhagen independent scene.

### [AFUK](https://www.afuk.dk/)

AFUK — Akademiet For Utæmmet Kreativitet ("The Academy for Untamed Creativity") — is a Copenhagen folk high school, performance venue and event house with a strong focus on new circus, physical theatre and cross-disciplinary work. Its stage is dedicated to the experimental, the emerging and unconventional artistic collaborations, programming Danish and international guest performances.

### [Dansehallerne](https://dansehallerne.dk/en/)

Dansehallerne is Denmark's national centre for dance and choreography, with its main home in Copenhagen's Carlsberg City District in a former brewery boiler room (Kedelhuset, opened August 2024), and a branch in Aarhus. It presents and co-produces Danish and international dance, hosts residencies, training, workshops and talks, and runs recurring platforms including NEW SH*T — a guest-curated showcase for new choreographic work — and the KORAL festival for children and families. Dansehallerne describes itself as a firm supporter of dance artists with a "strong sense of creative urgency."

### [HAUT](https://www.hautscene.dk/en)

HAUT is a hub for performing arts development located at Thoravej 29 in Nordvest. Through residencies, conversations and publications it creates space for artistic immersion and dialogue about the role and future of the performing arts, working closely with the Danish and international community to "create the opportunities we ourselves have missed."

### [Sort/Hvid](https://sort-hvid.dk/en/)

Sort/Hvid (Black/White) is an agenda-setting Copenhagen theatre housed in a renovated slaughterhouse in the Meatpacking District. Founded in 1972 as CaféTeatret and renamed in 2014, it stages art that examines current social taboos across genre and form; selected by the Danish Arts Council in 2020 as a venue for music-theatre development, a mandate running through 2029 and currently led by scenographer and director Nathalie Mellbye.

### [Sydhavn Teater](https://sydhavnteater.dk/)

Sydhavn Teater is a district theatre that grew out of Copenhagen's South Harbour neighbourhood, founded in 2012 by Mille Maria Dalsgaard and Søren Hansen, and now led by artistic director Tina Tarpgaard. The theatre works with a new generation of dance, performance and theatre artists, makes room for "other bodies and stories," and operates across several local stages — including the Kapel Scene in the former Østre Kapel, which becomes the theatre's permanent address in 2026.

### [Udviklingsplatformen for Scenekunst](https://udviklingsplatformen.dk/en)

Udviklingsplatformen for Scenekunst (the Development Platform for Performing Arts, "UP") is a Copenhagen-based resource centre dedicated to developing and qualifying the independent performing arts in Denmark and Danish independent work abroad. Based at Baldersgade in Nørrebro, UP offers mentorship programmes, residencies, seminars, advisory services, digital toolboxes and rehearsal-space rental, and has received operating support from the Danish Arts Foundation and the Bikuben Foundation since 2016.

### [Uafhængige Scenekunstnere](https://www.scenekunstnere.dk/)

Uafhængige Scenekunstnere (Independent Performing Artists) is a trade and interest organisation, founded in 2005, that works culturally and politically to recognise and improve conditions for professional, independent, self-producing performing artists in Denmark. It advocates for better production conditions, campaigns for open stages and rehearsal facilities, and promotes genre and aesthetic diversity, while representing the Danish independent field in Nordic cooperation with sister theatre centres in Sweden, Norway, Finland and Iceland. Membership is open to professionals with a board-recognised education or at least three years' work in the field.

_The former national choreographers' organisation De Frie Koreografer (The Free Choreographers) has since closed; its members advise people to join Uafhængige Scenekunstnere instead._

### [Dansekapellet](https://dansekapellet.kk.dk/en)

Dansekapellet is a municipal dance house in Bispebjerg/Nordvest, housed in a converted former chapel and crematorium from 1908 that was transformed in 2012 into one of Copenhagen's largest dance facilities. With five studios, it hosts classes, professional development, performances and residencies, and is the resident home of Uppercut Danseteater.

## Collectives

### [Danseatelier](https://www.danseatelier.dk/)

Danseatelier is an artist-driven space for dance and choreography in Nørrebro — a collective founded in 2015 by dancers and choreographers, out of a need for continuity, knowledge sharing and social and artistic support. Working through a feminist lens, the collective treats organising itself as ongoing research into collectivity, and unfolds through choreographic works, publications, workshops and residencies. Current members are: Ingvild Berteslen, Emilia Gasiorek, Olivia Riviere, Stine Frandsen, Nanna Stigsdatter, Karis Zidore, Snorre Elvin, Marlene Bonnesen and Sandra Liaklev Andersen.

### [Dance Cooperative](https://www.instagram.com/dance.cooperative/)

Dance Cooperative is an artist-run platform and association of 12 Copenhagen-based choreographers, dancers and performing artists, with a physical workspace at Valby Kulturhus and Grundtvigsvej 10B, Frederiksberg. The collective shares a commitment to art-making that explores queer, anti-racist, eco-sensitive and intersectional feminist tropes, organising the recurring EVES in-progress sharing series, and workshops and network events for practising artists.

### [KOMMA Performance Productions](https://kommaproductions.dk/)

KOMMA Performance Productions is a Copenhagen-based production cooperative in the independent field of dance and choreography, run by and for self-producing artists. Shaped around rotating working groups and member-driven initiatives — monthly meetings, a knowledge-sharing platform, choreographic afternoons and collective wage agreements — KOMMA co-produces performances, workshops and festivals; the cooperative was co-founded by Lara Vejrup Ostan, Nadja Bounenni and Joana Öhlschläger.

### [SPRÆNG danseteater](https://www.instagram.com/spraengdanseteater/)

SPRÆNG danseteater is a Copenhagen collective of seven dance artists — several of whom trained at the Royal Danish Ballet before moving into the independent field — co-founded in January 2020 by Helga Bach-Mortensen, Karoline Gammelgaard Jensen, Sarah Olivia Klitgaard, Katrine Vedsø, Mollie Næss-Schmidt, Jonatan Myhre and Alvilda Striim. The collective creates genre-crossing works that bring together contemporary dance, theatre and vocal performance.

### [Cadavre Exquis Collective](https://saraholiviaklitgaa.wixsite.com/danser/cadavre-exquis-collective)

Cadavre Exquis Collective is a Copenhagen collective of Enzo Hacquin, Madee Cole and Sarah Olivia Klitgaard, founded in January 2025 around a co-creation method inspired by the surrealist drawing game: leadership rotates continuously between artists throughout the process, distributing responsibility and emphasising intuition over self-censorship. Their debut work Aftermath — an abstract dance performance for three dancers exploring humanity's collective response to crisis and overconsumption — premiered 19–20 March 2026 at Basement, Copenhagen.

### [Hungry Eyes](https://www.hungryeyes.se/)

Hungry Eyes is a cross-disciplinary performing arts collective founded in 2023 by four graduates of the Conceptual Performing Arts programme at Malmö Theatre Academy, based across Copenhagen, Malmö and Stockholm. The collective creates contemporary, interdisciplinary and site-specific performance works exploring themes of labour, memory and collective experience, presenting in both Denmark and Sweden. Each year the group hosts a Scenekunstaften (Performing Arts Evening) — a public celebration showcasing works by members and collaborators, held in Copenhagen.

## Companies

### [Danish Dance Theatre](https://www.danskdanseteater.dk/en/)

Danish Dance Theatre (Dansk Danseteater) is Denmark's national company for contemporary dance, founded in 1981 and based at the Royal Danish Opera House in Copenhagen, home to a dozen full-time dancers. Since April 2023 the company has been led by Spanish choreographer Marina Mascarell, whose repertoire ranges from large stage productions and open-air events to intimate works for adults and children, with a commitment to sustainability under the Theatre Green Book.

### [Uppercut Dance Theater](https://en.uppercutdanseteater.dk/)

Uppercut Dance Theater is one of Denmark's first and longest-running modern dance companies, founded in 1982 by Cher Geurtze and Ann Crooset and currently led by artistic directors Stephanie Thomasen and Mark Philip. The diverse international ensemble fuses contemporary dance with hip-hop, breaking and physical acrobatics, performing for both adult audiences and children and young people, and based at Dansekapellet.

### [Kitt Johnson X-act](https://kittjohnson.dk/en/)

Kitt Johnson X-act, founded in 1992 and based at HUSET2450 in Sydhavnen, is one of Denmark's longest-running independent dance companies, built around the distinctive minimalist, body-driven universe of choreographer-dancer Kitt Johnson. Beyond its repertoire of solo, ensemble and site-specific works, X-act curates the site-specific MELLEMRUM concept and produces the biennial Copenhagen Circus Arts Festival (C!CAF).

## Festivals

### [CPH STAGE](https://www.cphstage.dk/en)

CPH STAGE is Copenhagen and Frederiksberg's major annual theatre festival, running for ten days each spring since 2013, with a programme of new Danish work, international touring productions, talks, debates, concerts, parties and the annual Reumert awards. It is a key meeting place for the Danish performing arts industry, with a strong programme for performance and dance alongside spoken-word theatre.

### [yes — dance biennale](https://yes-dancebiennale.dk/en-gb)

yes — dance biennale is a large-scale Copenhagen dance biennale created jointly by Dansehallerne, København Danser, Sydhavn Teater and Dansk Danseteater, running for the first time from 26 August to 28 September 2026. Across a full month it spreads performances, talks, workshops and artistic encounters across established stages, intimate studios and the city's public spaces, aiming to unite the field and capitalise on dance's current cultural momentum.

### [Toaster](https://toastercph.dk/)

Toaster is a curated guest-performance platform run jointly by Husets Teater and Den Frie Udstillingsbygning in Copenhagen, programming Danish and international companies and artists at the intersection of theatre, performance and visual art. Anchored in the Thoravej 29 community and supported by a four-year Danish Arts Foundation grant (2025–2029), Toaster sits on the steering group of the Nordic performing-arts network Yggdrasil and presented Denmark's first international Performance Biennale in April 2026, with 27 performances — including 12 new works — across Den Frie Udstillingsbygning and Husets Teater.

### [IPAF — International Performance Art Festival](https://warehouse9.dk/ipaf/)

IPAF (International Performance Art Festival) is a biennial festival produced by the multidisciplinary, queer-focused Copenhagen organisation Warehouse9, co-directed by Emma Castro Møller and Jørgen Callesen. Unfolding across the city, IPAF presents genre-blurring work engaging with identity politics, sexual expression and body representation; the most recent edition ran 19–22 March 2026.

### [Detour Dance Festival](http://www.detourfestival.com/)

Detour Dance Festival is a Copenhagen-based non-profit founded in 2012 by choreographer Sara Jordan, dedicated to supporting, empowering and uniting the Danish and international street and club dance community. Through performances, workshops and intergenerational exchange — and by creating space for Afro-Danish dancers — Detour bridges community and stage, honouring the roots of street and club dance cultures; it is presented in co-production with Dansehallerne.

### [Metropolis](https://www.metropolis.dk/en)

Metropolis is Copenhagen's site-specific and public-space performance platform, organised by Københavns Internationale Teater (KIT) — the city's interdisciplinary international performing-arts engine since 1980. Launched as a distinct programme in 2007, it runs as an annual summer season of performance and art in urban space and landscapes, plus the Performing Landscapes Lab, with strong ties to the EU's In Situ network for art in public space.

### [Copenhagen Circus Arts Festival (C!CAF)](https://ccaf.nu/en)

Copenhagen Circus Arts Festival (C!CAF) is a biennial festival presented by Kitt Johnson X-act that programmes high-quality international contemporary circus, focusing on works that erase the borders between circus and art. Recent editions have been hosted at Den Grå Hal in Christiania, drawing international companies and Copenhagen-based performers together.

### [Tårnby Park Performance Festival](https://www.taarnby.art/)

Tårnby Park Performance Festival is an annual community-rooted performance festival in Kastrup on Amager, founded in 2017 by performance artist Andreas Liebmann through Tårnby Park Studio in collaboration with the social-housing organisation Tårnbyhuse. The festival combines residency showings, international guest works, and citizen co-creation processeses.

## Spaces

### [Forsøgsstationen](https://www.forsoegsstationen.dk/en/)

Forsøgsstationen (The Lab Station) is an open workshop space for professional performing artists at the former Boulevard Teatret cinema on Sønder Boulevard 81, 1720 København V, co-directed by Lotte Faarup and Øyvind Kirchhoff. It offers continuous training, research projects, workshops and a professional library across theatre, music theatre, opera, performance, dance, new circus, animation theatre and fusions thereof. The space receives operating support from the Danish Arts Foundation's Project Support Committee for the Performing Arts.

#### [VA11a](https://va11a.com/)

VA11a is a collectively-run co-working space and dance studio at Siljangade 3, Amager, founded on 1 January 2019 by an international collective of around 20 members working across performance, dance, architecture, design, visual art, illustration and writing. The space offers a dedicated movement studio, meeting rooms, a kitchen and a shared courtyard — described by its members as "working alone together" — and is chaired by choreographer Lara Vejrup Ostan. It functions as everyday shared infrastructure for a wide cross-section of Copenhagen's independent performing artists.

### [Thoravej 29](https://www.thoravej29.dk/en)

Thoravej 29 is a cross-disciplinary work community in Copenhagen's Nordvest, opened in 2025 in a radically reused 1960s factory housing more than 190 people from 39 organisations working with art, social innovation, sustainability, democracy, digital innovation and politics. The building includes studios, workshops, a black box, exhibition rooms, recording studios and a public café, and is home to several key performing-arts organisations including HAUT and Toaster, among others.

### [Tårnby Park Studio](https://www.taarnbyparkstudio.dk/)

Tårnby Park Studio is an open performance platform and residency space founded by performance artist Andreas Liebmann in a former community basement at Randkløve Allé 102, Kastrup on Amager. Developed in partnership with the social-housing organisation Tårnbyhuse and supported by DDSKS and the Danish Actors' Association, the studio runs residencies, public interventions, site-specific co-creations with local residents and the annual Tårnby Park Performance Festival.

### [Basement](https://basement.kk.dk/)

Municipally-run cultural centre at Enghavevej 42, 1674 København V, regularly used as a black-box / industrial venue for performance evenings, dance and concerts. The space recently expanded its audience capacity to 230, allowing fuller use of the room. Run by Copenhagen Kommune.

### [Christianshavns Beboerhus](https://beboerhus.dk/)

Christianshavns Beboerhus is a 100% association-run, democratically governed community and culture house at Dronningensgade 34, founded through a peaceful occupation by 200 local residents in November 1975 and run since 1977 by the Christianshavns Beboerhusforening. With small permanent staff and around 100 volunteer activists, it programmes concerts, performances, communal meals and political and artistic activities. The house offers several affordable rehearsal rooms for rent — including Baglandet (42 m², mirrors, sound), Biffen (30 m², mirrors, sound) and Transitten (48 m², sound) — bookable by phone or in person at the café. It remains an emblem of grassroots, user-driven cultural space in Copenhagen.

### [Matriklen](https://matriklen.wordpress.com/)

Matriklen is a folkehus (community house) in the heart of Amager on Frankrigsgade, occupying an entire site with several small buildings around a spacious courtyard. Its back-house holds five small workshops rented to artists and craftspeople, and its main hall functions as an informal performance and event space — used, among others, by Hungry Eyes for their most recent Scenekunstaften. By day it operates as a work-café with refillable coffee; by evening it hosts pop-up performances, talks and communal gatherings.

## Blogs

### [bastard](https://bastard.blog/)

bastard is an independent platform and forum for experimental performing arts, performance art, choreography and cross-disciplinary practice, edited by Mette Garfield. It publishes criticism, articles, performance texts and a podcast in Danish, Norwegian and Swedish editions — in collaboration with SITE in Stockholm and Danseinformasjonen in Oslo — and works actively to strengthen the conditions of performing-arts criticism in Denmark.
